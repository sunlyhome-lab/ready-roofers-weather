Data Sources & Methodology

This report aggregates severe weather event data from the official NOAA Storm Prediction Center (SPC) Severe Weather Database (hail and damaging wind reports compiled from National Weather Service Storm Data).

Events are filtered to a 10-mile radius of the geocoded address, limited to the most recent 12 months of available data, and restricted to hail larger than 1.00 inch and wind speeds greater than 60 mph.

Note on data lag: Official SPC/NWS storm reports are quality-controlled and typically lag real-time observations by several weeks to a few months.
